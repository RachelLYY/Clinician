Biography

Yuwei Wu is an Associate Chief Physician in the Department of General Dentistry at the Second Outpatient Department, Peking University School and Hospital of Stomatology. He holds a doctoral degree in medicine from Peking University School of Stomatology, awarded in 2010.

From September 2011 to January 2013, he was a visiting scholar at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in the United States. His clinical practice focuses on comprehensive treatment planning for oral diseases and implant-supported rehabilitation for patients with partially or completely missing teeth.

Clinical expertise

Comprehensive treatment planning for periodontitis, dental caries, gingivitis and other oral diseases, together with implant-supported prosthetic rehabilitation for partial and complete tooth loss.
