Biography

Dandan Wang is an Attending Physician in the Department of General Dentistry at the Second Outpatient Department, Peking University School and Hospital of Stomatology.

She earned her doctoral degree in stomatology from Peking University School of Stomatology and completed standardized residency training as well as specialist training in oral and maxillofacial surgery.

Clinical expertise

Her practice encompasses minimally invasive extraction of impacted teeth and teeth requiring complex extraction, treatment of toothache and defects in tooth structure, dentoalveolar surgery, and comprehensive treatment planning for oral diseases.
